How to run it

Sets: tap a bubble when you finish a set. Tap a checked bubble to select it, then use − / + if you missed or beat the reps. Tap a selected bubble to uncheck it. The dashed + and − add or drop a set. Everything saves as you go.

Weight: starts at last time's weight. When every set hits the top of the range, the card says Add weight.

Effort: stop most sets 1–2 reps short of failure; take the last set of each exercise to failure.

Rest: tap Start after each set. The clock sets itself to 2:30 after the first big lifts and 1:30 on everything else; −15 / +15 to change it.

Deload: every 6–8 weeks, cut the sets in half for a week.
